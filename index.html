MORE THAN AN INSTRUMENT

A tradition of connection.

Mbira music brings together interwoven melodies and repeating themes. In Zimbabwe and Malawi, the craft of making and playing these instruments is passed between generations. This digital study is an invitation to listen and explore.

Its layout and synthesized voice are inspired by the instrument. Real mbiras have individual tunings, timbres, and playing practices; this simulator does not reproduce an authentic Nyamaropa tuning.
